Selected work

One event, two directions

A fortieth birthday in Delafield, themed around a summer beach the hosts had been quoting all year. Rather than guess at the mood, I designed two complete directions and let them pick — same two cocktails, two entirely different nights.

Both were built to print at full size for the bar and scaled down for table cards. If your party has a theme, a running joke, or a place that means something — that's the raw material. Tell me the story and it becomes the artwork.

The proof

This brand is the sample

Peyton Pours is my own project, designed end to end — the martini mark, the palette, the typography, the cards I hand out, the sign that goes to every event, and the page you're reading. Nothing here was bought from a template.

A cool grey glass holding a warm pink pour, with one lemon twist as the only note of contrast — then cream, blush, rose, signature pink and charcoal carried across every piece. Cormorant Garamond for display, Work Sans for everything else.
